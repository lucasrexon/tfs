How this was tested, and what it can't see
  • Every US-listed common stock (listed and delisted, no OTC) up 50%+ at 9:25 am, priced $1 or more, with 500,000+ shares traded before the open. Prices are split-adjusted so a reverse split never looks like a gap.
  • Trades fill at the next 1-minute bar's open after a signal, never at the signal price. Stops fill at the bar's open if price jumped past them, plus 3% extra. An overnight gap through a stop fills at the next open.
  • Costs: 0.5% slippage each way (minimum 1 cent), $0.005 a share commission each way, a $0.03 locate fee per share, and a 100% yearly borrow fee per night held. A short-sale restriction day adds 1% to the entry slippage.
  • Skipped: entries after 2 or more trading halts that day, and stocks listed less than 180 days.
  • Borrow availability on each day isn't in free data, which is why "Borrowable only" exists. Shares outstanding stands in for the float, so the real float rotation is higher.
  • This page shows the build year only. Rules were compared here; the next year stays sealed until 1 or 2 chosen rules are tested on it once.
  • Educational research, not financial advice. Short selling can lose more than the money in the account.
